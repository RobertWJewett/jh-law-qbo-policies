# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Jewett Law QBO-Clio Sync ("the Application")
**Operator:** The Law Office of Robert Jewett ("the Firm")
**Last updated:** July 26, 2026
**Contact:** robert@jewettlaw.net

## 1. What this Application does

This Application is a private, internal integration built by and for the Firm. It uses the official QuickBooks Online API and the official Clio Manage API to:

- read accounting transactions (expenses, bills, journal entries, payments) from the Firm's own QuickBooks Online company, and
- - read trust-ledger and billing records from the Firm's own Clio Manage account,
 
  - in order to help the Firm diagnose and correct bookkeeping discrepancies between the two systems.
 
  - ## 2. Whose data this involves
 
  - The Application only ever connects to accounts the Firm itself owns and controls: the Firm's QuickBooks Online company and the Firm's Clio Manage account. It is not installed by, or made available to, any other business or individual. Data processed by the Application may incidentally include information about the Firm's own clients (e.g., client names on trust transactions and invoices) to the extent that information already exists in the Firm's QuickBooks Online and Clio Manage records.
 
  - ## 3. How data is used
 
  - Data pulled from QuickBooks Online and Clio Manage is used exclusively for the Firm's own internal accounting review and reconciliation. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Firm's own bookkeeping.
 
  - ## 4. Data sharing
 
  - The Firm does not sell, rent, or share data obtained through the Application with any third party, other than:
 
  - - the Firm's own bookkeeping and accounting service providers, when engaged to assist with the Firm's books, and
    - - as required by law (e.g., in response to a valid legal process).
     
      - ## 5. Data storage and retention
     
      - Data pulled via the Application is stored locally within the Firm's own computing environment and is retained only as long as needed for the internal accounting review it supports. OAuth tokens used to authenticate to QuickBooks Online and Clio Manage are stored locally and are not transmitted to any third party.
     
      - ## 6. Security
     
      - Access to QuickBooks Online and Clio Manage is authorized via each platform's standard OAuth 2.0 flow. The Firm can revoke the Application's access at any time from within QuickBooks Online's or Clio Manage's own connected-apps settings.
     
      - ## 7. Changes to this policy
     
      - If the Firm changes how this Application uses data, this page will be updated accordingly.
     
      - ## 8. Contact
     
      - Questions about this policy can be directed to robert@jewettlaw.net.
