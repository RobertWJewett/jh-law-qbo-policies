# Jewett Law QBO-Clio Sync — Disconnected

**Application:** Jewett Law QBO-Clio Sync
**Operator:** The Law Office of Robert Jewett
**Contact:** robert@jewettlaw.net

If you are seeing this page after disconnecting, it means the Firm's QuickBooks Online company has been disconnected from this private, internal tool. The tool no longer has access to QuickBooks Online data until it is reconnected.

## How to reconnect

An authorized member of the Firm can restore access at any time by running the tool's authorization script again from within the Firm's own computing environment. That script opens QuickBooks Online's standard OAuth 2.0 authorization screen, where the Firm signs in and grants access again. See the Connect / Reconnect page for details.
